Umrah — field guide

01

Ihram

At the miqat Pillar

Intend umrah in your heart. That intention is . The cloth is not.

Key point is sunnah, not a condition. Intention alone makes you muhrim — begin the talbiyah after it and recite it often until tawaf.

Before intending Sunnah

  • . Trim nails, remove unwanted hair.
  • Perfume the body, not the cloth.
  • Two rak'ahs, if not a disliked time.
  • Men: and , unstitched.
  • Women: modest clothing, face and hands bare, feet covered.

The words

نَوَيْتُ الْعُمْرَةَ وَأَحْرَمْتُ بِهَا لِلَّهِ تَعَالَى

Nawaytul-'umrata wa ahramtu biha lillahi ta'ala

I intend umrah and have entered ihram for it, for Allah Most High. The intention in the heart is what counts — any language, no words needed. Saying it helps.

لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ

Labbayk Allahumma labbayk. Labbayka la sharika laka labbayk. Innal-hamda wan-ni'mata laka wal-mulk. La sharika lak.

Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am — You have no partner — here I am. All praise, favour and dominion are Yours. You have no partner.

Now forbidden

  • Cutting hair or nails. Perfume. Hunting.
  • Intercourse, its preliminaries, contracting marriage.
  • Men: stitched clothes, and covering the head. The face may be covered.

From the miqat to Makkah

  • Flying in: the miqat passes beneath the plane — be in ihram before it. Intend at the airport or when the crew announce the boundary (roughly 15–20 minutes before landing in Jeddah). Crossing it without ihram obliges a , unless you go back out to the miqat and re-enter.
  • On the road: keep the talbiyah going — rising, descending, meeting other pilgrims, after prayers. Men aloud, women quietly.
  • Sleep, eat, shower (unscented soap), and use the seatbelt, glasses, ring and money belt freely — none of this touches ihram. Men: mind the blanket over the head, and no fitted footwear covering the ankle bone.
  • Ghusl before entering Makkah is sunnah if you can manage it.
  • Enter the Masjid with the right foot: Allahummaghfir li dhunubi waftah li abwaba rahmatik — O Allah, forgive my sins and open for me the doors of Your mercy.

On first seeing the Kaaba

Du'a at the first sight is held to be answered — decide beforehand what you will ask for, raise your hands, and ask for it in your own words first. Then:

اللَّهُمَّ زِدْ هَذَا الْبَيْتَ تَشْرِيفًا وَتَعْظِيمًا وَتَكْرِيمًا وَمَهَابَةً، وَزِدْ مَنْ شَرَّفَهُ وَكَرَّمَهُ مِمَّنْ حَجَّهُ أَوِ اعْتَمَرَهُ تَشْرِيفًا وَتَكْرِيمًا وَتَعْظِيمًا وَبِرًّا

Allahumma zid hadhal-bayta tashrifan wa ta'ziman wa takriman wa mahabah, wa zid man sharrafahu wa karramahu mimman hajjahu awi'tamarahu tashrifan wa takriman wa ta'ziman wa birra

O Allah, increase this House in honour, reverence, nobility and awe — and increase those who honour and revere it, of those who come to it in hajj or umrah, in honour, nobility, reverence and righteousness.

03

Sa'y

0/7
At SafaLap 7 ends at Marwah.

One crossing = one lap · seven in total

Rules Pillar

  • Start at Safa, end at Marwah. Safa→Marwah is one lap.
  • Must follow a valid tawaf.
  • Wudu not required — only recommended.
  • Men walk briskly between the green markers. Idtiba' continues.
04

Halq

Cutting the hair Pillar

  • Three hairs is the minimum.
  • Shaving is superior for men.
  • Women take a fingertip's length from the ends.
Key pointThis is a pillar — no can stand in its place. You are not out of ihram until the hair is cut.

Order is itself a pillar

: ihram → tawaf → sa'y → halq. Ihram is now fully lifted.

No farewell tawaf is required for umrah in this school.
