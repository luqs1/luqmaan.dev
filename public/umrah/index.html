Umrah field guide

01

Ihram

Before the miqat Sunnah

  • Take a . Trim your nails and remove unwanted hair.
  • Put perfume on your body, not on the cloth.
  • Men: put on the and , two unstitched cloths.
  • Women: modest clothing. Don't cover the face (a cloth held away from it is fine) and don't wear gloves. Keep the feet covered.
  • Pray two rak'ahs, unless it is a time when prayer is disliked.

At the miqat Pillar

Make the intention for umrah in your heart. That intention is . The cloth is not.

نَوَيْتُ الْعُمْرَةَ وَأَحْرَمْتُ بِهَا لِلَّهِ تَعَالَى

Nawaytul-'umrata wa ahramtu biha lillahi ta'ala

I intend umrah and have entered ihram for it, for Allah Most High.

Any language works. Saying it helps, but the heart is what counts.

Then begin the (sunnah, not a condition) and recite it often until tawaf.

لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ

Labbayk Allahumma labbayk. Labbayka la sharika laka labbayk. Innal-hamda wan-ni'mata laka wal-mulk. La sharika lak.

Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am. You have no partner. Here I am. All praise, favour and dominion are Yours. You have no partner.

Flying inBe in ihram before the plane passes the miqat. Intend at the airport, or when the crew announce the miqat (about 15 to 20 minutes before landing in Jeddah). Entering ihram early is allowed.

Now forbidden

  • Cutting hair or nails.
  • Perfume.
  • Hunting.
  • Intercourse (it invalidates the umrah), and kissing or touching with desire.
  • Marrying, or marrying someone off as guardian or agent. The contract is void.
  • Men: covering the head with anything, including a blanket while asleep.
  • Men: clothing that encloses the body or a limb, such as shirts, trousers and socks (stitched or not).
  • Men: footwear that covers the heel or toe tips. If you have no sandals, khuffs cut below the ankle bone are allowed.

Still fine

  • Sleeping, eating, showering with unscented soap.
  • Seatbelt, glasses, a ring, a belt or money belt.
  • Men covering the face.
  • Being a witness to a marriage.

On the way to Makkah

  • Keep saying the talbiyah: going uphill and downhill, meeting other pilgrims, after prayers. Men aloud, women quietly.
  • Take a ghusl before entering Makkah, if you can (sunnah).
  • Enter the Masjid with your right foot and say: Allahummaghfir li dhunubi waftah li abwaba rahmatik. (O Allah, forgive my sins and open for me the doors of Your mercy.)

On first seeing the Kaaba

This du'a is said to be answered. Raise your hands and ask for what you decided on beforehand, in your own words. Then say:

اللَّهُمَّ زِدْ هَذَا الْبَيْتَ تَشْرِيفًا وَتَعْظِيمًا وَتَكْرِيمًا وَمَهَابَةً، وَزِدْ مَنْ شَرَّفَهُ وَعَظَّمَهُ مِمَّنْ حَجَّهُ وَاعْتَمَرَهُ تَشْرِيفًا وَتَكْرِيمًا وَتَعْظِيمًا وَبِرًّا

Allahumma zid hadhal-bayta tashrifan wa ta'ziman wa takriman wa mahabah, wa zid man sharrafahu wa 'azzamahu mimman hajjahu wa'tamarahu tashrifan wa takriman wa ta'ziman wa birra

O Allah, increase this House in honour, reverence, nobility and awe. And increase those who honour and revere it, of those who come to it for hajj and umrah, in honour, nobility, reverence and righteousness.

Passed the miqat without ihram?Go back to it and enter ihram there, unless you have an excuse such as danger or no time. If you're back before starting any rite, no is owed, even if you'd already entered ihram. Otherwise a dam is owed. Passing it deliberately is sinful.
02

Tawaf

Before you start

  • Conditions: wudu, a clean body and clothes, awrah covered.
  • Men: . Uncover the right shoulder for all seven circuits.
  • Stop the talbiyah.

Each circuit

  • Start level with the Black Stone. if you can reach it.
  • Kaaba on your left. Seven circuits, finishing at the Black Stone. Each one is a .
  • Stay outside the . If you cut through it, that circuit doesn't count.
  • Men: in circuits 1 to 3. If it's crowded near the Kaaba, do it further out, unless that puts you among the women. No dam if you miss it.

After the seventh circuit

  1. Men: cover the right shoulder.
  2. Pray two rak'ahs (sunnah) behind the , or anywhere in the Haram if it's crowded.
  3. Touch the Black Stone if you can, then go to sa'y. Men: uncover the shoulder again.
If wudu breaksRenew it and carry on from where you stopped. Don't restart. No is owed.

Contact in the crowd

  • Contact in the mataf is hard to avoid. In real hardship, Shafi'i scholars allow following al-Shafi'i's other opinion: the person touched keeps their wudu. This doesn't apply if you both collided.
  • Or follow another school's view, meeting that school's conditions for wudu.
  • Avoid crowding where you can.
03

Sa'y

0/7
At SafaLap 7 ends at Marwah.

One crossing = one lap · seven in total

Each lap Pillar

  1. On Safa: face the Kaaba, raise your hands, say the takbir, dhikr and du'a. Repeat three times. Tap Safa above for the words.
  2. Walk to Marwah. Men hurry between the green markers.
  3. On Marwah: the same as on Safa.

Safa to Marwah is one lap. Seven laps, finishing at Marwah.

Good to know

  • Sa'y must come after a valid tawaf.
  • Wudu is not required, only recommended.
  • Men keep the right shoulder bare (idtiba').
04

Halq

Cut the hair Pillar

  • Minimum: three hairs.
  • Men: shave the head (better) or trim.
  • Women: trim, never shave. A fingertip's length from all around the head is recommended.

Your umrah is now complete and ihram is fully lifted.

Key pointHalq is a pillar and comes last, after sa'y. The order itself, , is a pillar too. Until your hair is cut you are still in ihram. A cannot replace it.

Special cases

  • Doing hajj later on this trip, and your hair won't grow back in time? Trim now, shave at hajj.
  • No hair on the head? Nothing is owed. Passing a razor over the head is recommended.

Before you leave Makkah Wajib

  • Do the farewell tawaf (tawaf al-wada') when you are about to leave, as its own tawaf. Your umrah tawaf doesn't count.
  • It isn't part of umrah, but it is obligatory when leaving Makkah for qasr distance, or less if going home.
  • Left without it? Go back before reaching qasr distance. Otherwise a is owed.
  • Exempt: women in menstruation or post-natal bleeding.

Sources

Every point in this guide was checked against the Shafi'i works below, read in the OpenITI digital library (the corpus usul.ai is built on). Page numbers follow the printed editions the texts were digitised from, and may be off by one.

This is a list of sources, not a fatwa. Take questions about your own situation to a qualified teacher.

al-Nawawi, al-Majmu' Sharh al-Muhadhdhab. His long commentary, with the hadith evidence and gradings.
